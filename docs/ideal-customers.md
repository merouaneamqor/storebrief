# StoreBrief ideal customers

StoreBrief is likely the best fit for retailers with **multiple branches, repeatable store procedures, and a head-office or regional operations team**.

The same list is on the homepage and on [Who it's for](http://localhost:3001/resources).

## Strongest prospects

1. **Supermarkets and grocery chains** — store checks, promotions, cleanliness, food-safety tasks, and photo evidence.
2. **Fashion and clothing chains** — merchandising standards, campaign rollouts, new collection displays, and store visits.
3. **Beauty and cosmetics retailers** — product displays, launch campaigns, staff guidance, and compliance checks.
4. **Electronics and appliance stores** — product setup, promotional instructions, store audits, and issue reporting.
5. **Furniture and home-goods retailers** — showroom standards, display checks, delivery or maintenance issue reporting.
6. **Pharmacies and health-and-beauty chains** — operational checklists, staff communications, and store inspections.
7. **Convenience stores and fuel-station shops** — opening and closing checks, promotions, and site inspections.
8. **Sports and footwear retailers** — seasonal campaigns, visual merchandising, and store task tracking.
9. **Telecom and mobile-phone shops** — campaign instructions, display standards, and branch-level follow-up.
10. **Franchise networks** — consistent procedures and communications across independently operated locations.

## Adjacent opportunities

- **Quick-service restaurants and cafés:** daily checklists, launches, cleanliness, and location inspections.
- **Hotels and multi-site service businesses:** team tasks, inspections, and operational communications.

I’d start sales outreach with **grocery, fashion, beauty, and franchise groups** that operate many locations. They’re the clearest match for StoreBrief’s communications, campaigns, forms, checklists, and reporting features.
